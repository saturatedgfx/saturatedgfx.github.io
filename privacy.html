Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Inbox Tab Sorter is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, and it reaches only the owner's own Google account.

What it reads

It reads the sender, subject and inbox labels of recent messages. It does not read message bodies or attachments.

What it changes

It moves messages between inbox tabs. It creates, updates and removes the Gmail filters it made itself. It leaves all other filters alone.

Where data stays

Its sign-in token and its list of sender choices stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any other service. No data is used for advertising or to train any model.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to stop it

The owner can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's folder removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions go to the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
