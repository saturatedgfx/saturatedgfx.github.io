Inbox Tab Sorter

Inbox Tab Sorter is a small personal tool. It keeps one person's own mailbox tidy by moving mail into the right inbox tab.

It runs on its owner's own computer and signs in only to its owner's Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it does:

  • Reads the sender and subject of recent mail.
  • Moves each message to the inbox tab its owner chose for that sender.
  • Keeps a set of Gmail filters in step with those choices.
